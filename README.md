I build the layer between raw operational data and the decisions people actually make: warehouses that hold up under load, models that answer a real business question, and dashboards a manager can act on in thirty seconds.

Four years of that across telecom, pharmaceutical distribution, manufacturing and marketplace operations — most recently a data warehouse serving 500M+ records at Asiatech. M.Sc. in Industrial Engineering (Kharazmi University), which is where the optimization and forecasting side of my work comes from.

Right now I work with clients as a freelance BI engineer, and I'm open to full-time roles abroad, remote contracts, and BI training.

## What I work on

**Data platforms** — dimensional warehouse design, SSIS pipelines, incremental and API-sourced loads, data quality gates, query and model tuning.

**Analytics and reporting** — Power BI, DAX, SSAS Tabular models, SSRS/RDL, KPI definition and monitoring for sales, marketing and production teams.

**Decision science** — demand forecasting (Holt-Winters, SARIMA, XGBoost/LightGBM), LP and MILP planning models, stochastic optimization, A/B test design.

**Enterprise systems** — Microsoft Dynamics CRM implementation, process analysis in BPMN and UML, customer-facing portals.
